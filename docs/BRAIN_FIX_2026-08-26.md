## Deferred spine safety phase

Before powering the ESP32/servo layer again, change and bench-test the firmware
so boot remains disarmed and the robot cannot enter stand/home-rise until an
explicit, fresh operator command is received. The firmware must also own the
deadman timeout and safe neutral/output-disable state. This requires a separate
firmware compile and suspended-robot commissioning pass.

The Pi 5 RTC could not be persisted with `fake-hwclock` or `hwclock` because
neither utility is installed. Without reachable NTP or an RTC backup battery,
a full power loss may make the clock inaccurate again.
